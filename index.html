ProLyft

A workout tracker for your phone. Log your sets, follow a training plan, and see how far you have come.

  • Log every set

    Record weight, reps and rest as you train. Warm-ups, drop sets and failure sets each have their own mark.

  • Follow a plan

    Run a multi-week program or a single routine, and ProLyft writes the whole schedule onto your calendar.

  • See your progress

    Charts, a muscle map and personal records turn your logs into a picture of what you have actually done.

  • Train with friends

    Follow people you know, keep a weekly streak, and share a session as a card.

ProLyft is in testing and is not yet available to download. This page exists so that anyone can read what the app does and how it handles their data before installing it.
