Join the ProLyft beta

iPhone and Android · open this page on your phone

ProLyft is a strength-training log: pick or build a program, log your sets with the rest timer, and follow your records, muscle focus and recovery.

iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight, Apple's app for beta testing, from the App Store.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Open the invite on your iPhone, tap “Accept”, then “Install”.

    Open the TestFlight invite

If TestFlight says the beta isn't accepting new testers, it's full or not open yet. Please try again later.

Android

Use the same Google account as in Google Play for both steps.

  1. Join the tester group: on the group's page, tap “Join group”.

    Open the tester group

  2. Open the Google Play test page, tap “Become a tester”, then install ProLyft from Google Play.

    Open the Google Play test page

The Google Play link works once the beta build is live. If it says you're not a tester yet, please try again later.

What we ask

Use ProLyft for your real workouts and tell us what breaks, what confuses you and what's missing:

  • Telegram: the ProLyft Beta channel, for update notes and bug reports
  • Email: support@prolyftapp.com
  • In the app: Settings → Support & Legal → Write to us

What you get

The beta is free. Want to try Premium? Sign in once in the beta app, then ask us, and we'll switch it on for your account until the beta ends. No ratings or reviews needed, just honest feedback.

Privacy

This page uses no cookies and no tracking. The services linked on this page (the tester group, TestFlight, Google Play and the chat channels) run under their providers' own privacy policies. In the tester group only we can see your email address, and we delete the group when the beta ends. ProLyft itself is covered by our Privacy Policy and Terms.
